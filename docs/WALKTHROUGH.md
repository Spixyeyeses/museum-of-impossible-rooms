# The Museum of Impossible Rooms — walkthrough

This guide contains complete solutions and the ending. Use **H** in the game if you prefer a gradual hint. All chamber solutions below follow the implemented campaign, including its one-weight OPEN/HOLD/RELEASE finale.

## Controls and the court

Use **WASD** to walk, **Shift** to walk faster, and the mouse or arrow keys to look. Click the scene to capture the mouse; click-and-drag also works. **E** reads, uses, picks up, or places an index weight. **Q** sets a carried weight down. **J** opens the notebook and map; **Escape** pauses. There is no jumping, combat, timed failure, or fall damage.

Read **To the visitor** at the near end of the court, then enter exhibition **01**, on the left side. Each completed index illuminates the next numbered doorway. The ninth index reveals the final exhibition at the centre of the far wall. Walk around the court's central sculpture plinth to approach that final entrance.

Weights show their current size in the interface. Socket prompts show the requested size. A completed socket locks its weight; it cannot be accidentally removed. “Quarter” means one quarter of the starting measure: the normal 0.8 m index weight becomes 0.2 m.

## 01 — The Gallery of Two Norths

**Goal:** return the weight behind the glass to the entrance plinth.

1. Enter the bronze arch on the right, labelled **THE OTHER NORTH**. The doorway rotates your route into the long gallery.
2. Cross the gallery's far arch, **BEYOND THE GLASS**. You arrive behind the continuous glass in the starting room.
3. Take the weight and retrace the two connections: the enclosure arch first, then the gallery's entrance arch.
4. Place the 0.8 m weight on the entrance plinth.

**Return:** the **THE COURT** arch is behind the starting area. **Optional:** read **All points north** in the long gallery before returning.

**Why it works:** the two connected frames have different room positions and directions. The glass itself never opens.

## 02 — The Small Pavilion

**Goal:** move the west collection's weight to the east collection's plinth.

1. Enter the little pavilion's front door, **WEST COLLECTION**.
2. In the much larger archive, take the weight in the west half. The tall glass partition has no opening.
3. Return through the same archive door to the pavilion's exterior.
4. Walk around the pavilion on its right side, then enter its rear door, **EAST COLLECTION**.
5. Place the weight on the east plinth.

**Return:** leave through the east archive door. Outside, walk around the pavilion again to reach the main **THE COURT** arch. **Optional:** **A request for more drawers** is near the entrance end of the west archive.

**Why it works:** the small exterior is a short route between widely separated sides of the larger interior.

## 03 — The Scale Cabinet

**Goal:** fit the quarter-size weight beyond the low cabinet opening.

1. Take the normal weight in the studio before entering the measuring frame.
2. Cross **1 : 4**. Your body, reach, and carried weight become one quarter of their original measure.
3. Walk forward beneath the low cabinet lip. It physically excludes a full-size visitor.
4. Place the 0.2 m weight on **Quarter measure**.

**Return:** walk back beneath the lip and cross the measuring arch in reverse, **4 : 1**. You return to normal size. Then use **THE COURT**. **Optional:** **Life size** is in the original studio.

If you forgot the weight, simply reverse the arch, collect it, and try again. The connection never closes.

## 04 — The Unseen Garden

**Goal:** carry the winter weight into the summer conservatory.

1. Enter **WINTER**, take its weight, and return to the main garden.
2. Use the brass stand labelled **Release the arrangement**.
3. Turn fully around to face the entrance, leaving the destination arch behind you. Wait for the latch sound.
4. Turn back. The same arch now opens into **SUMMER**.
5. Carry the weight through and fill the summer plinth.

**Return:** use the summer return arch, then **THE COURT**. **Optional:** **Maintenance log** is in summer.

The stand releases one change; looking away by itself does not repeatedly shuffle the room. If you selected summer before retrieving the weight, use the stand again and look away to bring winter back. The frame will not change while you are watching it or standing very close to it.

## 05 — The Sightline

**Goal:** establish the perspective connection and retrieve its weight.

1. Stand inside the ivory **STAND HERE** mark near the entrance.
2. Look through the two partial bronze frames toward the centre of the far frame. From this spot, their outlines coincide.
3. Hold that view until the alignment indicator fills, about one quiet second. The real doorway opens and stays open.
4. Walk through it, take the weight in **The Completed Outline**, and return through the same connection.
5. Fill the original entrance plinth.

**Return:** use **THE COURT**. **Optional:** read **Here** in the room beyond the completed outline.

You do not need to keep aiming precisely while walking. If the charge will not begin, move back onto the mark and centre the far frame; standing elsewhere does not establish the connection.

## 06 — The Gravity Atrium

**Goal:** reach the plate on the other floor.

1. Use **Change the floor** at the central compass.
2. Allow yourself to settle onto the right-hand wall. That wall is now the floor beneath you.
3. Walk along it toward the elevated north arch labelled **THE OTHER FLOOR**. You can now move across what used to be the room's height.
4. Cross the arch and collect **The orientation plate** in the record gallery.

**Return:** cross the record gallery's arch back onto the wall floor. Use the compass beside this arch to restore ordinary gravity, settle onto the original floor, and return through **THE COURT**. **Optional:** read **Premature labels** in the record gallery.

This chamber collects a plate directly; it has no weight to carry or socket to fill. W/A/S/D continue to move across your current floor, and the camera and movement basis follow the change in down.

## 07 — The Hall of Measures

**Goal:** make a quarter-size weight without undoing the shrink on your return.

1. Pick up the weight.
2. Cross **1 : 2** into the small return gallery.
3. Leave through **UNMEASURED**, at the gallery's far end. This brings you back to the starting hall while keeping your half size.
4. Repeat: cross **1 : 2**, then return through **UNMEASURED**. You and the weight are now quarter size.
5. Enter the tiny **QUARTER COLLECTION** doorway on the left side of the hall and fill **One quarter** with the 0.2 m weight.

**Return and restore normal size:** leave the quarter collection. Enter **UNMEASURED** from the main hall to reach the return gallery without changing size, then leave that gallery through **2 : 1**. You are now half size. Repeat **UNMEASURED → 2 : 1** once more to regain full size. Use **THE COURT**.

**Optional:** **Same place** is in the unmeasured return gallery.

Reversing the measuring route immediately would undo each shrink. The useful loop has one measuring boundary and one neutral boundary. If you shrink too far, inverse loops or **R** restore a workable measure.

## 08 — Blind Transit

**Goal:** bring the correctly measured weight to conservation.

1. Enter **COLLECTION · 1 : 1** and take the full-size weight.
2. Return through the collection's rotated arch to the main gallery.
3. Use **Release the arrangement**, turn completely away from the destination frame, and wait for the latch.
4. Enter the newly revealed **CONSERVATION · 1 : 2** while carrying the weight.
5. Place the resulting 0.4 m weight on **Half measure**.

**Return:** cross conservation's arch in reverse to restore normal visitor size, then use **THE COURT**. **Optional:** read **A correction** in the full collection.

The same physical frame changes both its destination and its scale relationship. If you switched too early, return to the main stand and release another unobserved change to recover the collection route.

## 09 — The Ceiling Observatory

**Goal:** use a viewpoint on the wall floor and carry its weight into the record room.

1. Take the weight before using the compass.
2. Use **Change the floor** and settle onto the right-hand wall.
3. Walk along that floor to its ivory **STAND HERE** mark.
4. Look through the partial bronze outlines toward the far north frame. Hold until the connection settles.
5. Carry the weight through the high frame. The portal rotates you from wall-up into an ordinary floor-up record room.
6. Fill its 0.8 m plinth.

**Return:** cross the record-room arch back onto the observatory wall. Use the nearby wall compass, settle onto the original floor, and use **THE COURT**. **Optional:** **The three corrections**, in the record room, explains the final exhibition's relationships explicitly.

Looking at the frame from the ordinary floor cannot solve this chamber. The viewpoint's location and required up direction are both part of the condition.

## 10 — Exit Without Exterior

The finale asks for three persistent corrections: **OPEN**, **HOLD**, and **RELEASE**. There is only one carried weight. There is no hidden order code or guessed permutation.

### OPEN — the interior and its measure

1. Enter the small pavilion, **OPEN · THE INTERIOR**. Its interior exceeds the enclosure.
2. Take the index weight inside.
3. Carry it through **1 : 2 · OPEN** and fill **OPEN · half measure** with the resulting 0.4 m weight.
4. Walk around the filled plinth and leave through **2 : 1 · RETURN** at the opposite end of the room. You return to the main exhibition at normal size. The OPEN correction remains complete.

### HOLD — the unobserved arrangement

5. Use the now-available **Release the arrangement** stand in the main exhibition.
6. Turn completely away from the far destination frame until the latch sounds. This correction latches permanently.
7. Enter **HOLD · THE OTHER FLOOR**.

### RELEASE — the view from the other floor

8. Use **Change the floor**, settle onto the east wall, and walk along it to the ivory observation mark.
9. Align the partial frames toward **RELEASE · MORNING** until the connection settles.
10. Walk through the real exit doorway into the morning courtyard.
11. Read **Iona Vale, outside** near the departure desk, then use **Sign the departure ledger**. Iona has departed safely, and you record your own departure beneath hers.

The courtyard has a place set for you. Its return doorway remains open. You can go back to explore optional exhibits or use the notebook to return to the court; a completed visit stays completed.

## Recovery, saving, and common mistakes

- **Misplaced weight:** open **J → Save & recovery → Recall loose weights**. Loose weights return to their original stands and measures. Your visitor's current size stays as it is, so reverse the scale arch or recover before collecting a full-size weight if necessary.
- **Confusing scale or orientation:** press **R** to recover to the current exhibition entrance at ordinary scale and gravity. Loose weights return home; completed indexes, notes, settled perspective connections, and finale corrections are retained.
- **Need the hub:** the pause menu and notebook offer **Return to the court**. This is safe recovery, rather than a puzzle step you must discover.
- **Wrong socket size:** the prompt states your weight's size and the requested measure. The weight remains in hand. Choose the appropriate measuring direction and try again.
- **Over-shrinking in chamber 7:** reverse the loop using **UNMEASURED → 2 : 1**, or recover with R. Further shrinking is blocked at the supported lower bound; this does not end the visit.
- **Summer/conservation is visible but the weight is elsewhere:** use the release stand and look away again to restore the other arrangement. Recovery does not erase your discovered arrangement, so you may need this deliberate toggle after recalling a weight.
- **Alignment is not charging:** stand on the ivory mark, use the required floor orientation, and aim through the far frame. Looking away after it settles is safe.
- **Continue later:** the game saves locally after important changes. **J → Save & recovery → Save to a file** exports a visit; the same tab can restore it. See `INSTALL.md` for the browser/address rules governing local saves.
- **Start over:** **Begin a new visit…** is separate from recovery and asks for confirmation. Export your current visit first if you want to preserve it.
